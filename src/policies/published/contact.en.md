# Contact

You can also [send feedback](/feedback) without signing in. Describe your issue or suggestion and optionally choose a screenshot and reply email. Signed-in users can follow responses in [My feedback](/account/feedback). For an existing paid order, use [Orders & refund requests](/account/refunds). Feedback is not live chat.

For help with PosterTiler, privacy requests or rights concerns, email **[support@postertiler.com](mailto:support@postertiler.com)**.

PosterTiler is operated by **mileschen**, an individual based in **Mainland China**, trading as PosterTiler.

## Help us understand the problem

Tell us which tool you used, your browser, paper size, finished dimensions and what happened. Include the error message where relevant. Start with a description; sending the original source image or PDF is optional. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or unnecessary personal information.

## Files and privacy

Files selected in the free tools stay in your browser. If you choose to email an attachment, it becomes part of your support correspondence and follows the separate email practices described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), including our Cloudflare-hosted mailbox and possible AI-assisted reply drafting.

Read our [Terms of Use](/terms) and [printing guides](/guides) for tool limits and printing instructions.
