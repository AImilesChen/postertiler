## Current release: test payments

Stripe checkout is currently in test mode. No real money is charged, and sandbox credits cannot be exchanged for cash. Refund requests and refund calculations currently demonstrate the review workflow using test orders. They do not enable real-money sales. The voluntary rules below are the rules adopted for eligible credit purchases when live sales are offered; the applicable terms must also be shown before live checkout.

## Unused credits and the 14-day voluntary window

For an eligible unused credit purchase, request a refund within 14 days of confirmed payment. If a pack has been partly used, the unused portion is calculated at the pack's actual purchase price, without repricing completed tasks at the higher single-task price:

**Amount paid × refundable unused credits ÷ credits originally purchased.**

The amount is rounded to the nearest cent and cannot exceed the payment's remaining unrefunded amount. For example, a $5.99 five-export pack with two exports used and three unused gives a $3.59 reference refund. Previously refunded credits, credits reserved for active tasks and credits already held for an approved refund are excluded. Cloud PDF credits and AI credits are separate.

Consumption is allocated to paid orders on a first-purchased, first-used basis. An AI task may use credits from more than one AI pack. Failed or canceled tasks release their reservation, and repeated downloads of the same available result do not consume new credits. Historical usage reconstructed before this ledger was introduced is labelled for operator review. A missing reliable payment date or an inconsistent ledger requires manual review rather than an invented eligibility decision.

The original payment-processing fee is not subtracted from this proportional customer refund calculation. Any required tax adjustments and non-excludable remedies must be handled separately where applicable.

## Service failures, duplicate charges and exceptions

A technical failure or a blocked safety check releases the task's reserved credits. This is a credit release, not a cash refund. Contact us if a charge was duplicated, the purchased service could not be delivered, or the delivered service was defective or materially different from what was promised. These issues are reviewed separately from the voluntary 14-day unused-credit window.

Successful AI processing is not automatically refundable merely because an artistic result is not to your taste. You may still request a review. We do not promise that AI enhancement restores authentic missing detail or that every image will be suitable for a particular print size.

Nothing in this policy limits applicable statutory cancellation, withdrawal or remedies for defective or undelivered services. The voluntary window is not an automatic refusal of those rights, and clicking Pay is not a blanket waiver.

## Request, review and payment status

Sign in and open [Orders & refund requests](/account/refunds). Choose an order you own and explain the issue. You can also contact support with the order reference; do not send card numbers or passwords. Submitting a request does not issue a refund or guarantee approval. The reference amount can change if credits are used before approval.

When the operator approves a refund, the corresponding available credits are held so they cannot also be spent. Approval does not move money. Refunds are completed separately through Stripe, normally to the original payment method. Verified refund events update the payment total, remove corresponding credits and release the matching hold without applying the same adjustment twice. If a refund is canceled or fails, contact support so the operator can verify Stripe before releasing a hold. Bank and payment-network timing can differ; a recorded Stripe adjustment is not a promise that funds have already reached your bank.

We retain an audit of the calculation and review. You can see your request, response, approved amount, payment adjustments and any reserved refund credits in your account. Changes in payment state and additional spending require the operator to refresh a stale calculation before approving it.

## Available files

Cloud and AI working files and authenticated result downloads expire 24 hours after task creation. Save your result during that period. Ordinary scheduled file expiry does not itself undo a successfully delivered task, but does not remove remedies where the promised delivery or availability was not provided. Free local poster tools remain free and do not require purchasing credits.
