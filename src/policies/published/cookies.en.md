# Cookie and Analytics Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

PosterTiler is operated by **mileschen**, an individual based in **Mainland China**. Contact **[support@postertiler.com](mailto:support@postertiler.com)** with privacy questions.

## Current browser storage

The free tools process selected images and PDFs in your browser. Their source files and previews are not saved to persistent browser storage by the application. Files you download remain wherever you choose to save them.

| Storage | Identifier | Purpose | Lifetime | Provider |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| localStorage | `postertiler-analytics-consent-v1` | Remember a choice made through Cookie settings | 180 days after the latest choice, or until you clear it | PosterTiler |

The current release has no configured Google Analytics 4 ID, loads no GA4 tag and sends no GA4 measurement requests. It does not use advertising or session-recording tags. Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and may use infrastructure cookies when its security features require them; these are separate from Google analytics and do not contain your selected poster files. See [Cloudflare's cookie explanation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

## Your choice

Open **Cookie settings** in the footer to review or change the stored preference. Analytics acceptance is disabled while analytics is unconfigured. Rejecting analytics does not affect image selection, settings, local processing or PDF downloads.

When analytics is introduced, the existing controls require acceptance before loading its code or sending measurements. Withdrawal stops future collection, removes analytics cookies under our control and reloads the page to unload the analytics code. It does not automatically erase earlier data held by an analytics provider.

You can clear localStorage and cookies through your browser. Clearing storage removes the saved preference and may cause the site to ask again when analytics is enabled.

## Planned search and visitor measurement

Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are planned for checking indexing and search performance of public pages. They are separate from GA4 visitor measurement. We do not submit private poster files or download tokens for indexing.

Before enabling GA4, we will update this policy with its actual cookie inventory and account retention settings. The implementation excludes source images, PDFs, file names, email addresses, support messages and private download links from analytics events. Preparing a PDF is not recorded as proof of successful physical printing.

For more information and requests, read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) or email support@postertiler.com.
